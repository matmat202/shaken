Coda · Why

The fault is the reason the town is there

The geologist James Jackson calls it a fatal attraction. Faults lift mountains, and mountain fronts hold springs and rivers. Faults also open the passes trade routes follow. Villages grew there, and some have become megacities, most of them only in the last fifty years.
